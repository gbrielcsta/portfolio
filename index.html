About Me

Introduction

Mechanical Engineering undergraduate student at UFSJ, my academic journey has been marked by three years of participation in a university team focused on the development of UAVs for the SAE Brazil Aerodesign competition. I worked directly in the areas of aerodynamics, CFD, performance, stability, control and project integration. I was a scientific initiation scholarship holder, researching and conducting numerical simulations of turbulent flow around wind turbines for two years. Currently, I am the flight mechanics manager at Del-One UFSJ, a project focused on developing drones for operations in transmission lines.
